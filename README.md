### What I Do
I love taking ambiguous, messy problems and transforming them into clear, production-grade machine learning solutions. Whether it's wrangling terabytes of time-series data, designing robust evaluation frameworks for LLMs, or running causal inference to measure true incrementality — I thrive on the full end-to-end journey: from exploratory analysis in a notebook to reliable, monitored pipelines that deliver consistent value.

What excites me most is the **challenge**. The harder the problem (noisy labels, concept drift, tight latency/cost budgets, sparse data, interpretability demands), the more satisfying it is to crack. I genuinely enjoy debugging why a model performs differently in staging vs prod, optimizing inference to shave off 30% latency without losing accuracy, or figuring out why a seemingly perfect offline metric fails online.

### How I Approach Projects
1. **Deep problem understanding first** — I spend real time with stakeholders/business context before writing any code.  
2. **Iterate fast, validate ruthlessly** — Quick prototypes → strong baselines → rigorous experimentation (causal, sequential testing, uncertainty quantification).  
3. **Production mindset from day one** — Feature engineering that scales, monitoring for drift & performance, cost-aware optimization, CI/CD for models.  
4. **Communicate clearly** — Notebooks are for exploration; final deliverables include clean code, docs, dashboards, and business summaries.  
5. **Learn continuously** — I read papers, reproduce SOTA results, and contribute small fixes to open-source tools I use daily.
